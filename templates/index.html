COMPFEST is an annual one-stop IT event run by students of the Faculty of Computer Science, Universitas Indonesia. As the biggest IT event in Indonesia, it has been held annually since its very first event in 2009. Since then, it has evolved to become a core part of the Faculty of Computer Science’s culture. Every year, we strive to deliver a different core message and theme with the aim of educating the Indonesian community on the development of technology. (source: compfest.id)
